🧠
Why 2 separate ESP32 boards?
The camera uses heavy DMA buffers. If motors and camera share one chip, the motor interrupts crash the camera buffer.
📡
What does the Radar detect?
The HLK-LD2410C sends 24GHz microwaves. It can detect a person breathing from 6 meters away, even through walls.
🛰️
What does GPS give us?
Two numbers: Latitude and Longitude. These are the exact coordinates we push to Firebase so the map on the dashboard updates.
📏
Bot 2's Dead Reckoning:
Bot 2 has no GPS or encoders. It estimates its position using motor runtime × speed. This is called Dead Reckoning.

🔴
Why is Video kept Local?
A video frame is ~50KB. Uploading that to Firebase 15 times per second = 750KB/s. Firebase would crash and lag by 10 seconds.
🟢
Why are Numbers sent to Cloud?
GPS is just 2 numbers (14.23, 72.84) = about 40 bytes. That's nearly invisible to Firebase and arrives in under 300ms globally.

What are WebSockets?
Normal websites ask the server "Any updates?" every few seconds. With WebSockets, Firebase instantly PUSHES updates to the browser the millisecond data changes. Zero delay.
🎨
What is Glassmorphism?
A modern UI design language using frosted-glass panels, neon borders, and dark backgrounds. Used by Apple, Tesla, and military dashboards for clarity under stress.

Producer-Consumer Pattern:
Thread 1 grabs frames as fast as possible. Thread 2 processes AI on the latest frame only. Old frames are thrown away to prevent lag buildup.
🧠
What is YOLOv8s?
YOLO = "You Only Look Once." It scans the entire image in a single pass to find objects. The 's' means 'small' model — optimized for speed while keeping accuracy above 80%.
📦
Why deque(maxlen=2)?
If the AI takes 80ms per frame but camera sends at 30fps, frames pile up. The deque automatically drops old frames so the AI always processes the FRESHEST image, not a 3-second-old one.
🌐
Why Flask re-streaming?
After YOLO draws bounding boxes on each frame, Flask serves the annotated video as a new MJPEG stream. The dashboard just points an <img> tag at it.

Defense Architecture:
If internet fails, Bot 2 still maps locally. If camera fails, radar still tracks. If laptop fails, Firebase still stores data. Every layer has a fallback.
