================================================================================
  CONFIRMED PIN MAP  ★ SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH ★
================================================================================

  MOTOR DRIVER (L298N)
  ─────────────────────
  IN1  →  GPIO 27       Left motor direction A
  IN2  →  GPIO 26       Left motor direction B
  IN3  →  GPIO 25       Right motor direction A
  IN4  →  GPIO 33       Right motor direction B
  ENA  →  GPIO 14       Left motor PWM speed
  ENB  →  GPIO 32       Right motor PWM speed

  ENCODERS (Corrected after diagnosis)
  ─────────────────────
  Left  A  →  GPIO 21   (INPUT_PULLUP)
  Left  B  →  GPIO 22   (INPUT_PULLUP)
  Right A  →  GPIO 18   (INPUT_PULLUP)
  Right B  →  GPIO 19   (INPUT_PULLUP)
  Encoder VCC  →  ESP32 3.3V
  Encoder GND  →  ESP32 GND

  ULTRASONIC (HC-SR04)
  ─────────────────────
  TRIG  →  GPIO 5
  ECHO  →  GPIO 4       ← via 1kΩ/2kΩ voltage divider (5V→3.3V safe)
  ⚠ ECHO divider:  ECHO → 1kΩ → GPIO4 → 2kΩ → GND

  SERVO (SG90)
  ─────────────────────
  Signal  →  GPIO 13
  VCC     →  External 5V (NOT ESP32 3.3V)
  GND     →  Common GND

  ⚠ PIN CONFLICT NOTE:
    Old notes (info1.txt) had ECHO→GPIO18, Servo→GPIO19.
    Those conflict with encoder pins. UPDATED to GPIO4 and GPIO13.

================================================================================
  CONFIRMED MOTOR DIRECTION LOGIC
================================================================================

  leftMotor(+speed)   → IN1=LOW,  IN2=HIGH   (forward)
  leftMotor(-speed)   → IN1=HIGH, IN2=LOW    (backward)
  rightMotor(+speed)  → IN3=LOW,  IN4=HIGH   (forward)
  rightMotor(-speed)  → IN3=HIGH, IN4=LOW    (backward)

  TURN LEFT:   leftMotor(-spd), rightMotor(+spd)
  TURN RIGHT:  leftMotor(+spd), rightMotor(-spd)

================================================================================
  POWER WIRING
================================================================================

  External 6V battery (+) → L298N VS (marked +12V)
  External 6V battery (-) → L298N GND
  L298N GND               → ESP32 GND     ← COMMON GROUND
  L298N 5V output         → ESP32 VIN     (if confirmed safe on your module)
  ESP32 3.3V              → Encoder VCC

  DO NOT connect 6V directly to ESP32 3.3V or encoder VCC.
  DO NOT power motors from ESP32 VIN.
